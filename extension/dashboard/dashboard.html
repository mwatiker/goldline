Setup and connection help
  1. Start a match (the loading screen is fine).
  2. Open this link. Your browser will say the connection isn't private.
  3. Click Advanced, then Proceed to 127.0.0.1 (unsafe).
  4. Come back to this tab. It connects on its own within a few seconds.

Tip: If the link says the page can't be reached, the game hasn't started loading yet. Try again once it has. If Goldline stops connecting later, repeat these steps.

Why do I have to do this?

Goldline reads your match data from the League of Legends client on your computer (127.0.0.1). Your browser doesn’t recognize its certificate, so it shows a warning.

Goldline has no accounts or tracking and sends nothing to the developer.
